  • Radio Equipment Regulations 2017 (UK, SI 2017/1206)
  • RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU, as amended by EU 2015/863
  • RoHS Regulations 2012 (UK, SI 2012/3032)
  • EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542
